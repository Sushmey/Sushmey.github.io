I'm pursuing my Master's in Computer Science at CU Boulder, following about 2 years of experience working as a software developer. I've worked with various technologies and frameworks such as Python, Java, SQL, Spark, Hive, Django, Express, etc.

This summer, I interned at eBay, building an observability platform that fuses and normalizes fragmented user-journey signals into diagnostic timelines for eBay Live's bidding and session systems. Before that, I worked at Wells Fargo, building data processing frameworks, and interned at Barclays. I have also contributed extensively to Zulip Terminal, an open-source terminal-based chat application.
